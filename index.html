About

I am a mathematician and mathematics educator at NC State University. My work centers on undergraduate mathematics teaching, online and hybrid course design, accessibility, assessment, instructional technology, and the changing role of generative AI in mathematics education and research.

Current Work

  • Mathematics education and course design: developing and coordinating undergraduate and graduate mathematics courses, with particular attention to assessment, accessibility, and sustainable instructional systems.
  • Generative AI: studying and presenting on how AI changes mathematical teaching, assessment, research practice, and faculty work.
  • Mathematical research: geometric dynamical systems, currently interested in existence and uniqueness questions for singular p-Laplacian equations.
