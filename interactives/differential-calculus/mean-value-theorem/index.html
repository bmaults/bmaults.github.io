The Mean Value Theorem: A Tangent Line Parallel to the Secant Line

If a smooth curve goes from the point at a to the point at b, somewhere in between the tangent line is parallel to the secant line joining the two ends.

Questions to think about

  1. Can you choose a and b so there is more than one such point c?
  2. What happens when the two endpoints are at the same height? This special case is Rolle’s theorem.
