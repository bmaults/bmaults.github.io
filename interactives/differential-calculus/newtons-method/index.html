Newton’s Method: Tangent Lines That Find Where a Curve Crosses the Axis

Newton’s method finds where a curve crosses the axis. Draw the tangent line at your guess. Where that line meets the axis is the next, better guess.

xn+1 = xn − f(xn) / f′(xn)

Questions to think about

  1. What goes wrong if the first guess is where the tangent line is flat?
  2. How quickly do the guesses settle down? Compare the early steps to the later ones.
