Differential Calculus

Interactive pictures of the main ideas of differential calculus. Drag the sliders and watch the geometry change.

Derivatives and tangent lines

  • From Secant Line to Tangent Line

    Slide a second point toward the first and watch the secant line turn into the tangent line.

  • The Derivative as the Slope of the Tangent

    Move a point on a curve and see the slope of the tangent become the height of the graph of f′.

  • The Mean Value Theorem

    Find the tangent line that is parallel to the secant line joining two endpoints.

  • Zooming In on a Curve

    Zoom in on a smooth curve until it looks like its tangent line, and see why a corner is different.

  • Newton’s Method

    Follow tangent lines to where a curve crosses the axis.

  • Taylor Polynomials

    Approximate sine, cosine, exponentials, and logarithms by polynomials, and see the radius of convergence.
