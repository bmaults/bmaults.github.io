The Derivative as the Slope of the Tangent: Graphing f and f′

The derivative at a point is the slope of the tangent line there. Move the point and watch the slope become the height of the lower graph.

Questions to think about

  1. Where is f′ equal to zero, and what does the graph of f look like there?
  2. Where f is steepest, what does the graph of f′ look like?
