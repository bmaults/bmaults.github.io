Spring-Mass System with Damping: Underdamped, Critically Damped, and Overdamped

A mass on a spring with a damper. Change the stiffness, the damping, and the mass, and watch how the motion and its phase portrait change.

Questions to think about

  1. What happens to the motion as you increase the damping from zero? Find the damping where it just stops oscillating.
  2. How does the phase portrait change when the system goes from underdamped to overdamped?
