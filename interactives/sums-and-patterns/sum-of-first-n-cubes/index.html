The Sum of the First n Cubes: 1³ + 2³ + ... + n³ = (1 + 2 + ... + n)²

The side of the square is 1 + 2 + ⋯ + n. Each L-shaped layer has width k. Add layers one at a time.

Questions to think about

  1. Can you prove this identity with induction?
  2. Why does the L-shaped layer of width k have area k³? Compare the squares with sides 1 + 2 + ⋯ + k and 1 + 2 + ⋯ + (k − 1).
