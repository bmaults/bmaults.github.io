Factoring x² + bx + c with Algebra Tiles

Factoring a trinomial means arranging its tiles into a rectangle. The sides of the rectangle are the factors. Slide to arrange the tiles, and change p and q to build different rectangles.

Questions to think about

  1. Which tiles make the term x², which make the middle term, and which make the constant term?
  2. Why is the constant term the product of p and q, but the middle coefficient the sum of p and q?
  3. Look at a pile of tiles with just one x² tile, some x tiles, and some unit tiles. How could you tell whether they can be arranged into a rectangle?
