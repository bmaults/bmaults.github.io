Factoring Formulas

Pictures that show why the formulas for multiplying and factoring are true. Move the sliders and watch the pieces. Each page has a few questions to think about.

Multiplying and squaring

  • Multiplying Binomials with an Area Model (FOIL)

    A rectangle cut into four pieces shows where the four terms of FOIL come from.

  • The Square of a Binomial: Why (a + b)² = a² + 2ab + b²

    A square cut into four pieces shows where the middle term comes from.

Completing the square

  • Completing the Square with Rectangles

    Cut a strip in half, glue the halves to a square, and see what is missing.

Factoring

  • The Difference of Squares: Why a² − b² = (a − b)(a + b)

    An L-shaped region is cut apart and slid into a rectangle.

  • Factoring x² + bx + c with Algebra Tiles

    Arrange tiles into a rectangle, and the sides of the rectangle are the factors.

  • Factoring Sums and Differences of Cubes with SOAP

    A 3D picture of boxes cut apart and reassembled shows where the factors come from.
