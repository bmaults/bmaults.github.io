The Difference of Squares: Why a² − b² = (a − b)(a + b)

Cut a small square out of the corner of a larger square. Then cut the L-shape that is left into two pieces and slide them together.

Questions to think about

  1. Why does the area stay the same when the pieces are rearranged? What are the sides of the final rectangle?
  2. Which piece did we turn, and why does it fit exactly?
  3. Why is there no similar picture for a² + b²?
