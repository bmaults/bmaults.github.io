Multiplying Binomials with an Area Model: FOIL and Squares

Multiplying two binomials is finding the area of a rectangle cut into four pieces, and each piece is one term of FOIL. Choose \((a + b)(c + d)\) to change all four side lengths. Choose \((a + b)^2\) to make the rectangle a square.

Questions to think about

  1. Where does each of the four terms of FOIL come from in the picture? Why are there exactly four?
  2. In the square version, two of the pieces are the same size. Where does the \(2ab\) come from?
  3. A common mistake is \((a + b)^2 = a^2 + b^2\). Which pieces does this leave out?
  4. What would the picture look like for \((a + b)(c + d + e)\)? How many pieces would there be?
