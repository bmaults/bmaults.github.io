Multiplying Binomials with an Area Model (FOIL)

Multiplying two binomials is finding the area of a rectangle cut into four pieces. Each piece is one term of FOIL. Change the sliders to change the sizes of the pieces.

Questions to think about

  1. Where does each of the four terms of FOIL come from in the picture? Why are there exactly four?
  2. What would the picture look like for (a + b)(c + d + e)? How many pieces would there be?
  3. Make a = c and b = d. What kind of rectangle do you get, and which pieces are equal?
