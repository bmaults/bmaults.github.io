The Square of a Binomial: Why (a + b)² = a² + 2ab + b²

Why is (a + b)² not just a² + b²? Cut a square with side a + b into pieces and see.

Questions to think about

  1. Why are two of the four pieces the same size, and where does the 2ab come from?
  2. A common mistake is (a + b)² = a² + b². Which pieces does this leave out?
  3. How would the picture change for (a + b + c)²? How many pieces would there be?
