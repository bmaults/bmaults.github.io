Synthetic Division and the Remainder Theorem

Divide a polynomial by x − a one step at a time. Choose a polynomial, set a, and press Next step. The graph shows that the remainder is the value of the polynomial at a.

Questions to think about

  1. Which values of a give remainder zero? What do those values look like on the graph?
  2. Why do you need a zero in the table for a missing power of x?
