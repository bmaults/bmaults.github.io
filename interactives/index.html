Interactive Math Demonstrations

Free interactive pages for understanding mathematics, organized by topic. Each page runs in the web browser and has open-ended questions to think about.

Topics

  • Conic Sections

    Eight demonstrations of the geometry of the circle, ellipse, parabola, and hyperbola: slicing a cone, Dandelin spheres, the focus and directrix, sums and differences of distances, paper folding, reflection, and orbits.
