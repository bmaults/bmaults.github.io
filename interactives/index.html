Interactive Math Demonstrations

Free interactive pages for understanding mathematics, organized by topic. Each page runs in the web browser and has open-ended questions to think about.

Topics

  • Conic Sections

    Eight demonstrations of the geometry of the circle, ellipse, parabola, and hyperbola: slicing a cone, Dandelin spheres, the focus and directrix, sums and differences of distances, paper folding, reflection, and orbits.

  • Factoring Formulas

    Visual demonstrations of why the formulas for multiplying and factoring work: FOIL, squares of binomials, completing the square, difference of squares, trinomials, and sums and differences of cubes.

  • Sums and Patterns

    Visual demonstrations of why sum formulas work, including sums of integers, odd numbers, and cubes, and the Pythagorean theorem.

  • Integral Calculus

    Riemann sums, the mean value theorem for integrals, and both parts of the fundamental theorem of calculus.

  • Differential Calculus

    Secant and tangent lines, the derivative graph, the mean value theorem, linear approximation, and Newton’s method.

  • Differential Equations

    Damped spring-mass systems, simple harmonic motion, and the pendulum, with graphs and phase portraits.

  • Algebra and Precalculus

    Complex numbers, quadratics and the discriminant, and function transformations.

  • Trigonometry

    Angle sum and difference, double-angle, and half-angle formulas from geometric pictures.
