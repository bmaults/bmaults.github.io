Riemann Sums: Approximating the Area Under a Curve with Rectangles

The area under a curve can be approximated by rectangles. More, thinner rectangles give a better approximation.

Questions to think about

  1. Which endpoints give an underestimate for the rising curve? Which give an overestimate?
  2. Why do midpoints and trapezoids usually do better than endpoints?
