Improper Integrals: When an Infinite Area Is Finite

Can an area that goes on forever, or up to a point where the function blows up, still be finite? It depends on how fast the curve shrinks.

Questions to think about

  1. What happens right at p = 1? Does the area level off?
  2. Why does a larger p make the curve shrink faster, and why does that matter for the area?
