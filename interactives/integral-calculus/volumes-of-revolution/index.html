Volumes of Revolution: Slicing a Solid into Disks and Washers

Spin a region around the horizontal axis to make a solid. Use the first slider to watch the region turn, and the number of slices to see how the solid is built from thin disks (or washers). The dashed gray line is the axis of revolution.

Questions to think about

  1. As the number of slices grows, what happens to the stack of disks?
  2. Why is the face of a washer a ring instead of a full circle?
  3. Which slice has the largest face? Which has the smallest?
